I remember the first time I held a handful of raw, unspun fiber from the heirloom seeds. It felt like 'woven air' of antiquity—a caliber of fiber that has no modern equivalent. Watching the hands of a master weaver—hands that carried the muscle memory of five thousand years— I recognised the beauty I was called to restore.

The last century chose the mediocrity of speed, trading the biological soul for industrial convenience. The rise of industrial uniformity was a hollow victory; in our pursuit of the immediate, we unknowingly forfeited the excellence that once made our textiles immortal.

Felis Onca is the restoration.

We returned to the Primordial Seed. We work in the patient cadence of the Amber Charkha, where every thread is birthed by the human hand, preserving the natural character that give a fabric its life.

Beauty is inseparable from dignity. My mission is the restoration of sovereignty—honoring the final custodians of a vanishing lineage. By honoring our master artisans with a 15% premium, we ensure that sovereignty is primary thread of every garment I sign.
